# Work Handoff

Use a handoff only for work that spans people, AI tools, or sessions. Store the
completed handoff under `doc/plans/YYYY-MM-DD-<topic>.md`. Small tasks do not
need one.

## Objective

State the requested outcome and what is explicitly out of scope.

## Accepted Decisions

List only decisions confirmed by the project owner or formal documentation.

## Current State

List the branch, relevant files, completed changes, and any uncommitted work.

## Verification

Record commands actually run and their results. Do not infer success.

## Remaining Work

List concrete next actions, unresolved questions, and known risks.

## Context To Load

List only the documents the next contributor or AI needs. Do not instruct it to
scan the entire repository or documentation tree.
