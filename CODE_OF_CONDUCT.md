# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the KhaiKang community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and feedback.
- Focus criticism on ideas and work, not people.
- Respect privacy and do not share personal or confidential information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, deliberate
intimidation, or publication of private information without permission are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report Code of Conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through
GitHub. Reports will be reviewed fairly and handled as confidentially as
practical. Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict
participation when this policy is violated.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1:
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html>.
